3. A typo

count(happy, hemisphere)
# A tibble: 4 × 2
  hemisphere     n
  <chr>      <int>
1 both           5
2 north         92
3 noth           4
4 south         21
n_typo <- sum(happy$hemisphere == "noth")
happy <- mutate(happy, hemisphere = if_else(hemisphere == "noth", "north", hemisphere))
count(happy, hemisphere)
# A tibble: 3 × 2
  hemisphere     n
  <chr>      <int>
1 both           5
2 north         96
3 south         21

“noth” is a typo for “north”, in 4 countries. Without the fix they would be left out of every summary of the northern hemisphere. Always count() a categorical column before using it.

Part 4: Summaries

8. Describe the happiness score

happy |>
  summarise(
    mean   = mean(happiness_score),
    median = median(happiness_score),
    p25    = quantile(happiness_score, 0.25),
    p75    = quantile(happiness_score, 0.75),
    min    = min(happiness_score),
    max    = max(happiness_score),
    sd     = sd(happiness_score)
  )
# A tibble: 1 × 7

Part 5: The report

11. Tidy the document

A heading per part: ## Import, ## Cleaning, ## Manipulation, ## Summaries, ## Results. Chunks the reader does not need to see get #| echo: false, for example:

```{r}
#| echo: false
happy <- clean_names(happy)
```

12. Results with numbers

What you write in session2.qmd:

We have data on `{r} nrow(happy)` countries.
The mean happiness score is `{r} round(mean(happy$happiness_score), 1)` out of 10.
The happiest region is `{r} by_region$region[1]`, with a mean of `{r} round(by_region$mean_happiness[1], 1)` over only `{r} by_region$n[1]` countries.
The country that drinks the most is `{r} top_drinkers$country[1]`: `{r} top_drinkers$total_per_capita[1]` servings per person per year.
In total, `{r} n_typo` countries had the hemisphere typo "noth".

What the reader sees after rendering:

We have data on 122 countries. The mean happiness score is 5.5 out of 10. The happiest region is Australia and New Zealand, with a mean of 7.3 over only 2 countries. The country that drinks the most is Czech Republic: 665 servings per person per year. In total, 4 countries had the hemisphere typo “noth”.

13. Render, change, render again

north <- filter(happy, hemisphere == "north")
nrow(north)
[1] 96
round(mean(north$happiness_score), 1)
[1] 5.6

With only the northern hemisphere, the first sentence would say 96 countries and a mean of 5.6, with no number typed by hand. This is why reports are written with inline code.
